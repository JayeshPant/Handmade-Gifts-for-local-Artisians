Our Artisan Promise

Why Gifting With Us Feels Special

We bridge authentic rural artisan guild families directly to your doorstep with love, dignity, and sustainable values.

100% Handcrafted

Every creation is hand-formed, hand-carved, or hand-poured without synthetic factory mass-machinery.

Eco-Friendly & Safe

Natural river clay, GMO-free soy wax, unbleached cotton, and zero single-use plastics in all packages.

Fair Trade to Karigars

Over 78% of proceeds go directly to rural artisan families and women-led craft cooperatives.

Bespoke Gifting Touches

Custom handwritten calligraphy notes, wax seals, and seed-paper plantable tags included with every order.

Handcrafted Care Guide
  • Wash with warm water and soft lemon/soap nut rinse. Avoid abrasive metallic scrubbers.
  • Allow natural unglazed terracotta to air-dry thoroughly in sunlight before storing.
  • Store away from damp closed cabinets to maintain its fresh natural clay aroma.
  • Treat with pride as an heirloom piece created with generations of craft lineage.
